## Human reviewers (待录)
_Names of Tibetan medical practitioners, Sanskritists, and translators who contribute human validation, chapter revisions, and terminology refinements will be recorded here as pull requests are merged._

To contribute:
1. Open an issue describing which chapter or term you propose to revise.
2. Fork, edit, and open a pull request.
3. Follow the terminology conventions in `translations/sushruta-samhita/STYLE.md`.
4. All contributions accepted under CC BY-SA 4.0.

## Source acknowledgements
- **English source**: Kaviraj Kunjalal Bhishagratna (1907), *An English Translation of the Sushruta Samhita, Based on Original Sanskrit Text*. Public domain.
- **Text access**: [wisdomlib.org](https://www.wisdomlib.org/hinduism/book/sushruta-samhita-volume-1-sutrasthana)
- **Terminology base**: *Aṣṭāṅgahṛdayasaṃhitā* (Tibetan: ཡན་ལག་བརྒྱད་པའི་སྙིང་པོ་བསྡུས་པ།) and *rGyud bZhi* (རྒྱུད་བཞི།) — the two foundational medical corpora of Tibetan medicine.
- **Referenced but not used as source** (still in copyright): Priya Vrat Sharma edition (with Ḍalhaṇa commentary); G. D. Singhal edition.
