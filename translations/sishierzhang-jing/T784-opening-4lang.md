### English · Beal 1871 — **no English exists for this preface in Beal**: he translated the 明 recension, which omits it (note ⑧); his note 1 on p. 190 (above) refers the reader to the JRAS for the dream. The column below is therefore a Sarasvatī draft, not Beal.

> ⟨བརྟག⟩ *Sarasvatī draft:* Of old, the Han emperor Xiaoming dreamed at night that he saw a divine man, his body of golden colour, a sun's light at his neck, flying before the palace hall; in his heart he rejoiced and was greatly pleased by it. The next day he asked his assembled ministers: "What god is this?" There was a man of wide learning, Fu Yi, who said: "Your servant has heard that in Tianzhu there is one who has attained the Way, called the Buddha; he rises lightly and can fly — this must surely be that god." Thereupon the emperor understood, and at once sent the envoys Zhang Qian, the Gentleman-of-the-Household of the Feathered Forest Guard Qin Jing, the Academician's disciple Wang Zun and others, twelve men, to the country of the Great Yuezhi, to copy out the Buddha's sūtra in forty-two sections, which was in the fourteenth stone case; and he raised and established a stūpa-temple. Thereupon the Way and the Law spread abroad and everywhere Buddhist temples were built. Those from afar who submitted, were transformed and wished to become his subjects could not be counted; within the realm there was peace and purity; the classes of sentient beings received his grace and have relied on it to this day without cease.

### 现代汉语 ⟨བརྟག⟩ Sarasvatī 机器初稿
> ⑧从前汉孝明皇帝夜里梦见一位神人，身体呈金色，颈项有日光，飞在殿前；心中欣悦，非常喜欢。第二天问群臣："这是什么神？"有博通之人傅毅说："臣听说天竺有得道的人，号称'佛'，身轻能飞，大概就是那位神吧。"于是皇上醒悟，立即派遣使者⑨张骞、羽林中郎将秦景、博士弟子王遵等十二人，到大月支国抄取佛经四十二章——它藏在第十四个石函中——并登即建立塔寺。于是道法流布，处处修建佛寺。远方之人归化、愿为臣妾的，不可胜数；国内清平安宁，含识之类蒙受恩惠、依赖至今，不曾断绝。
