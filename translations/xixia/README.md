# xixia · 西夏文 Tangut (Khara-Khoto)

**Tier 1 · Rank 1** — the highest-priority sub-branch after Pan's 2026-11-02 orphan-canon brief.

## Why Tier 1 Rank 1

- Few living readers. Fewer than a few dozen scholars worldwide can read Tangut fluently as of 2026.
- No full-text corpus input. ~370 separate Buddhist titles (Kychanov 1999 catalogue) across ~768 Khara-Khoto manuscripts at IOM RAS alone, essentially none fully digitised as text.
- Physical fragility. Tenth-to-thirteenth-century silk and paper, stored at IOM RAS (Kozlov 1909 expedition finds), BL, National Library of China, Ningxia Social Sciences collection, etc.
- **Preserves lost Chinese and Tibetan originals**: early Kagyu/Sakya tantra, Huayan, Chan materials whose Chinese or Tibetan source is otherwise unknown.

## License trap — read before using images

The main digital collection is **EAP140/1** at [eap.bl.uk](https://eap.bl.uk/collection/EAP140-1) (210 manuscripts, 25,955 TIFF images from IOM RAS). It is marked **"research purposes only"** and the EAP reuse policy forbids large-scale image reuse or commercial use without IOM RAS permission.

→ **Sarasvatī must NOT mirror EAP140 images.** We may:
- Link to specific EAP140 shelf-marks from our transcription files.
- Download single JPEG images through the Universal Viewer for in-session reading (scan-first discipline).
- Cite exact folio ranges in our records.

→ **For CC BY-SA 4.0 publication of images**, we need genuinely public-domain scans. Candidates to investigate:
- Gorbachëva & Kychanov 1963 facsimile plates (RUS public domain by 70-year rule? → confirm)
- Nishida Tatsuo 1977 Kyoto facsimile of Tangut Huayan (likely still copyrighted → license query)
- Chinese holdings at Ningxia / 国家图书馆 (status unclear → outreach letter 2026-11-02)
- Any CC-licensed facsimile published after 2000 (check openly)

Until a public-domain image source is confirmed, our first xixia sample will be **text-only** with a cross-reference to EAP140 shelf-mark.
