## Critical review notes

- **Chapter 4 (embryology):** month-by-month fetal development includes concepts (Dauhṛda, Sattvaja/Pitṛja/Mātṛja components) that do not map 1:1 with Tibetan medicine; retained as Sanskrit transliterations pending expert review.
- **Chapter 6 (marmas):** 107 vital anatomical points including sizes, positions, and consequences of injury. **Every marma is marked** ⟨བརྟག་དགོས། བོད་སྨན་མཁས་པས་ཞིབ་བརྟག་བྱ་དགོས།⟩ **and must be validated by a Tibetan medical practitioner before authoritative use.**
- Approximately 60 disputed terminology items are collected at the end of the raw file under `# དོགས་གནས་བརྡ་ཆད།` — including Kloma (མཆིན་མཛོད vs གློ་མཛོད), Kalā seven-tissue names, marma finger-width unit conversion, and Bhadraśrī (ཙནྡན་དཀར་?).
