# abhidharma-vinaya-chinese · 汉文部派论律 (孤本)

**Tier 1 · Rank 3** — Pan's 2026-11-02 orphan-canon brief.

## Why Tier 1 Rank 3

The complete Sarvāstivāda, Dharmaguptaka, Mahīśāsaka, and Mahāsāṃghika Abhidharma + Vinaya traditions survive **only in Chinese**. The Sanskrit originals of (nearly) all of them are lost. The Pāli parallel is in a different tradition and cannot substitute.

**Fully digital, almost zero modern translation.** CBETA has electronic text of every work below. Scanned images are on IA (`taisho-tripitaka`). But: *none of the following have a complete modern translation in any language* (as of 2026):

- **《大毗婆沙论》 Abhidharma Mahāvibhāṣā (T1545, 200 juan)** — the great Sarvāstivāda compendium
- **《发智论》 Jñānaprasthāna (T1544)** + the six legs (六足论)
- **《顺正理论》 Nyāyānusāra by Saṅghabhadra (T1562)**
- **《大智度论》 Mahāprajñāpāramitā Śāstra (T1509, 100 juan)**
- **《解脱道论》 Vimuttimagga (T1648)** — the Pāli original is lost
- 四分律 Dharmaguptaka Vinaya (T1428); 五分律 Mahīśāsaka (T1421); 摩诃僧祇律 Mahāsāṃghika (T1425); 十诵律 Sarvāstivāda (T1435)
- 四阿含 (T1 Dīrgha, T26 Madhyama, T99 Saṃyukta, T125 Ekottara) — each from a different school

The partial translations that *do* exist: Buswell/Jaini/Reat etc. in the Encyclopedia of Indian Philosophies, Potter 1999; a few dissertations on single chapters; Japanese kokuyaku; scholarly article-level selections. No complete book-length modern rendering of any of the above into English, modern Chinese, or Tibetan.
