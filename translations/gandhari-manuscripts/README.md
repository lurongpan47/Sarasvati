# gandhari-manuscripts · 犍陀罗语写本 Gāndhārī Manuscripts

**Tier 1 · Rank 4** — Pan's 2026-11-02 orphan-canon brief.

## Why Tier 1 Rank 4

Gāndhārī Buddhist manuscripts, written in Kharoṣṭhī script on birch bark c. 1st c. BCE – 3rd c. CE, are **the oldest surviving Buddhist writing**. Collections:

- **British Library collection** (BL, 1994 acquisition): ~80 fragments. Studied by the Early Buddhist Manuscripts Project (Salomon, Allon, Baums, Glass et al.) and published in the *Gandhāran Buddhist Texts* (GBT) series. CKM 18 and CKM 27 catalogue entries contain the BL Dharmapada (16+25) and Avadānas.
- **Senior collection**: ~24 scrolls, slightly later. Published as GBT volumes.
- **Schøyen collection**: birch bark, palm leaf, vellum. Mixed Gāndhārī and Buddhist Sanskrit.
- **Bajaur collection**: Pakistani-held.
- **Dīrgha-āgama-type Gāndhārī** (private + academic study).

Fragments are physically fragile. Images are published; raw manuscripts are irreplaceable.

**Why it is a Tier 1 orphan**: some sūtras preserved on these scrolls are **the only surviving witness** of a lost text in a lost Prakrit dialect of an extinct early Buddhist school (Dharmaguptaka in some cases). The Khotan Dharmapada is a different manuscript (ca. 3rd c. CE, Senart 1897 Barua–Mitra 1921 edition — already partially covered in `translations/gandhari-dharmapada/`).
