## Terminology notes (draft, ⟨བརྟག⟩)

- **寂滅宮 (jŏkmyŏl kung)**: "palace of quiescent cessation" — classical rendering of *nirvāṇa* as a palace/dwelling, standard in East Asian Buddhist literature.
- **火宅門 (hwataek mun)**: "gateway of the burning house" — alludes to the parable of the burning house in the *Lotus Sūtra* ch. 3 (Kumārajīva T.262).
- **三毒 (samdok)**: the three poisons — greed (貪 *tṛṣṇā/rāga*), hatred (瞋 *dveṣa*), delusion (癡 *moha*).
- **四蛇 (sasa)**: the four snakes — the four elements (earth, water, fire, wind) that constitute the body, dangerous when clung to; a standard metaphor in Chinese and Korean Buddhist literature derived from Āgama/Nikāya materials.
- **五欲 (ogok)**: the five sense-desires — form, sound, smell, taste, touch.
- **芬皇寺 (Punhwangsa / Bunhwangsa)**: temple in Kyŏngju where Wŏnhyo was ordained; still standing today (ROK National Treasure).
