## ⚠ Erratum (2026-09-23) — both source columns re-read against the page images; neither was what it claimed

The 2026-09-13 version of this file (commit `ca3fb61`) labelled its Pāli column "PTS edition" and its English column "Lord Chalmers 1932, *Buddha's Teachings*". **Neither column was that text.**

- The **English** ("This is what should be done by one who is skilled in good, and who wishes to attain that state of Peace: Let him be able, upright, perfectly upright …") shares **no line** with Chalmers. Chalmers translates the whole Sutta-Nipāta into short unrhymed verse; his Sn 1.8 is headed "Sutta 8. Goodwill" and begins *"When Peace is won, th' adept / in welfare needs to prove / an able, upright man"*. The old column was a modern prose rendering of the chanting-book type; its actual source has not been identified and is not asserted here — and because such renderings are typically still in copyright, it had to come out on asteya grounds as well as satya.
- The **Pāli** was not the PTS text either. Its orthography (`Karaṇīyamatthakusalena`, `yantaṃ`, `cassa`, `suhujū`, `khuddamācare`, `sabbasattā`, `pāṇabhūtatthi`, `vanavasesā`, `yeva adiṭṭhā`, `Byārosanā`, `katthaci na kañci`, `puttamāyusā`, `sabbalokasmi`, `averamasapattaṃ`, `yāvatāssa`, `brahmametaṃ vihāramidhamāhu`, `vinaya gedhaṃ`, `jātuggabbhaseyya puna reti`, semicolons at the half-verse) is that of the Burmese Sixth-Council (Chaṭṭha Saṅgāyana / VRI-CST) recension as it circulates online, not Andersen & Smith 1913. The PTS text separates the sandhi (`Karaṇīyam atthakusalena`, `c' assa`, `yāvat' assa`), reads `ujū ca sūjū ca`, `mudu`, `khuddaṃ samācare`, `sabbe sattā`, `vā anavasesā`, `ye vā addiṭṭhā`, `vyārosanā`, `katthacinaṃ kañci`, `sabbalokasmiṃ`, `averaṃ asapattaṃ`, `vineyya`, `gabbhaseyyaṃ punar etī ti`, marks sandhi-lengthening with a circumflex (`nâtimaññetha`, `nâññamaññassa`), prints the anusvāra as ṃ (dot below), and carries a variant apparatus (Bᵃ Bⁱ Cᵏ Cᵇ = the two Burmese and two Sinhalese MSS, Fsb. = Fausbøll 1885), which is now carried under each verse.

Both columns are now the printed text. Chalmers' line breaks are kept as verse with his marginal verse numbers; his printer's thin space before `;` `:` `!` is reproduced as an ordinary space. PTS footnote numerals are carried as superscripts where they fall; the PTS right-margin stanza numbers are given as ⟦1.⟧ … ⟦10.⟧. Substantive differences, was → is:

## Verse 7 · As a mother her only child  (Sn 149)

### English (Chalmers 1932, p. 37; marginal [149])

> for all that lives be thine,

### Chinese (机译)

## Verse 8 · Boundless love toward all the world  (Sn 150)

### Pāli (PTS 1913, p. 26; verse 150 · stanza 8)
> [F. 27]. Mettañ ca sabbalokasmiṃ¹⁰  
> mānasam bhāvaye aparimāṇaṃ  
> uddhaṃ adho ca tiriyañ ca  
> asambādhaṃ averaṃ⁹ asapattaṃ.  ⟦8.⟧

PTS p. 26 nn. 9–10: ⁹ Bⁱ -am (the same note is attached to *puttaṃ* 149a and *averaṃ* 150d). — ¹⁰ Bᵃⁱ -mi. — `[F. 27].` stands in the left margin — the editors' marginal concordance to Fausbøll's edition, as understood; their preface was not read for this audit.

### English (Chalmers 1932, p. 39; marginal [150])
> —an all-embracing love  
> for all the universe  
> in all its heights and depths  
> and breadth, unstinted love,  
> unmarred by hate within,  
> not rousing enmity.

### Chinese (机译)
> 应以无量之慈心，遍满一切世间；上、下、四方，无碍、无怨、无敌。

### Burmese (机译 ⟨བརྟག⟩)

### Thai (机译 ⟨བརྟག⟩)

## Verse 9 · Standing, walking, sitting, lying  (Sn 151)

### Pāli (PTS 1913, p. 26; verse 151 · stanza 9)
> Tiṭṭhaṃ caraṃ nisinno vā¹¹  
> sayāno vā¹² yāvat' assa vigatamiddho,  
> etaṃ satiṃ¹³ adhiṭṭheyya,  
> brahmam etaṃ vihāraṃ¹³ idha-m-āhu.  ⟦9.⟧

PTS p. 26 nn. 11–13: ¹¹ Bⁱ va. — ¹² Bⁱ *omits* vā. — ¹³ Bⁱ -m (attached to both *satiṃ* and *vihāraṃ*).

### English (Chalmers 1932, p. 39; marginal [151])

> with all your might on this ;

### Chinese (机译)

### Burmese (机译 ⟨བརྟག⟩)

### Thai (机译 ⟨བརྟག⟩)

## Verse 10 · Never come again to any womb  (Sn 152)

### Pāli (PTS 1913, p. 26; verse 152 · stanza 10)
> Diṭṭhiñ ca anupagamma  
> sīlavā dassanena sampanno  
> kāmesu vineyya¹⁴ gedhaṃ,  
> na hi jātu gabbhaseyyaṃ punar etī ti  ⟦10.⟧

PTS p. 26 n. 14: ¹⁴ Bⁱ vinaya (*cf.* 1098ᵃ). — Colophon follows: `METTASUTTAṂ NIṬṬHITAṂ.`

### English (Chalmers 1932, p. 39; marginal [152])
> Ignoring sophists' views,  
> the good—with insight filled,  
> and purged of appetite  
> for sensuous delights—  
> shall never see rebirth.

### Chinese (机译)
> 不堕邪见，具戒具见，调伏诸欲贪着——如是之人，不复入胎矣。

### Burmese (机译 ⟨བརྟག⟩)

### Thai (机译 ⟨བརྟག⟩)
