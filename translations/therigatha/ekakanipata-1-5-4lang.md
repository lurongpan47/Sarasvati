## Conventions

- **Pischel 1883**: long vowels carry a **circumflex** (â î û), anusvāra is **ṃ** (dot below), retroflexes ṇ ṭ ḍ ḷ, palatal ñ — all kept as printed, not normalised to IAST macrons. Verse pādas end with `|` and `||` with the verse number between double bars (`|| 1 ||`); each psalm closes with a centred attribution (`Puṇṇâ. ||`) or, for vv. 1–2, a two-line prose frame. The apparatus is a run-on paragraph: *line-number, comma, sigla, reading*, entries divided by em-dashes; **the numbers are the lines of the page** (1 = the invocation, 2–18 = the text), counted here and matched (e.g. `2, BLP therike` ↔ line 2 *therîke*; `15, om. cdd.` ↔ line 15 `Tissâ. ||`). Sigla from Pischel's Preface (p. [121], read from the OCR layer, not the leaf): **L** Phayre MS, India Office, Burmese script · **P** Bibliothèque Nationale, fonds Pali 91, Burmese · **S** Subhūti's MS, Sinhalese · **B** Subhūti's MS, Burmese, dated Sakkarāj 1128 · **C** text embodied in the commentary MS (Paramatthadīpanī) · **cdd.** = codices · `B 1. hd.` = B, first hand (so Pischel elsewhere: `B 1. hd. … 2. hd.`). `°` marks the abbreviated part of a word.
- **Mrs Rhys Davids 1909**: her spaced punctuation before `!` `;` `:` and inside single quotes (`‘ Ford ’`) is **kept** as printed; superscript note numerals in their printed position; italics hers; the verse number `(1)` … `(5)` set right after a wide space; diacritics as printed (Puṇṇā, Muttā, Tissā, Rāhu, Āsavas; `Bhikkhunī`). Her verse line breaks are kept; the prose psalms are not carried (see the notes under each verse for what the prose says the verse is).
- **Drafts**: 现代汉语 and བོད་ཡིག are Sarasvatī machine drafts ⟨བརྟག⟩ made from the Pāli, consulting Mrs Rhys Davids only for sense; names are transcribed (Muttā 蔑陀 / གྲོལ་མ་ by meaning in the note; Puṇṇā 富楼那 / གང་མ་; Tissā 帝沙 / ཏི་ཥྱཱ). Every line of these two columns awaits a named human reader.

## Invocation and verse 1 · sukhaṃ supâhi therîke … sukkhaḍâkaṃ va kumbhiyaṃ

### Pāli (Pischel 1883, p. 123 [leaf 137], lines 1–5)
> **THERÎ-GÂTHÂ.**
>
> Namo tassa bhagavato arahato sammâsambuddhassa.
>
> sukhaṃ supâhi therîke katvâ coḷena pârutâ |  
> upasanto hi te râgo sukkhaḍâkaṃ va kumbhiyaṃ. || 1 ||  
> itthaṃ sudaṃ aññatarâ therî apaññâtâ bhikkhunî  
> gâthaṃ abhâsitthâ ti. ||

*Read at 1.2× in two half-strips (lines 1–3) and two more (lines 4–6). Title centred over a short double rule, with circumflexes on both* Î *and* Â*. Line 2 opens lower-case* sukhaṃ*;* therîke *with a circumflex on the* î *(the apparatus shows BLP had* therike*);* coḷena *with* ḷ*;* pârutâ *two circumflexes; single bar. Line 3* sukkhaḍâkaṃ *with* ḍ*, circumflex and final* ṃ*; period, then* || 1 ||*. Lines 4–5 (the frame) indented, the second more deeply;* apaññâtâ*,* bhikkhunî *(circumflex), no punctuation at the end of line 4;* ti. || *closes line 5. No superscript numerals anywhere on the page.*

Pischel, apparatus to lines 1–5 (foot of p. 123, lines 1–3 of the apparatus): 1, L adds ti.—2, BLP therike, PS colena, P pâdutâ.—3, P the, S dukkha°, P ca.—4, L añatarâ, P añatarâ theri, B asañâtâ, L apañâtâ, P asañâkâ, S asaññatâ.—5, P °ni.

*Read at 1.5× (whole block, halves), 2.5× (each line in thirds) and 4×. Entry 2: one P-reading only after* PS colena, *—* P pâdutâ. *(two circumflexes; 2.5×; the 1.5× read's "P p[?]a" was the seam between halves). Entry 3:* P the *plain, no circumflex;* dukkha° *with the degree sign. Entry 4: five readings with a single* ñ *(*añatarâ, añatarâ theri, asañâtâ, apañâtâ, asañâkâ*) and the sixth,* S asaññatâ*, with double* ññ *(2.5×, settled).*

## Verse 2 · Mutte muccassu yogehi … anaṇâ bhuñjâhi piṇḍakaṃ

### Pāli (Pischel 1883, p. 123, lines 6–9)
> Mutte muccassu yogehi cando Râhuggaho iva |  
> vippamuttena cittena anaṇâ bhuñjâhi piṇḍakaṃ. || 2 ||  
> itthaṃ sudaṃ bhagavâ Muttaṃ sikkhamânaṃ imâya  
> gâthâya abhiṇhaṃ ovadati. ||

*Read at 1.2× (lines 4–6 and 7–9 strips).* Mutte *and* Muttaṃ *capitalised,* Râhuggaho *capitalised with circumflex;* anaṇâ *with a single* ṇ *in the text (the apparatus has BCP* aṇaṇâ*);* piṇḍakaṃ *ṇ ḍ ṃ; period then* || 2 ||*; frame indented as in vv. 1;* ovadati. || *closes line 9.*

Pischel, apparatus to lines 6–9 (apparatus lines 3–4): 6, P mucchassu, S idha.—7, S °muttona, BCP aṇaṇâ, S anna.—8, P puttaṃ, PS °mânâyaṃ.—9, P imâyaṃ ga°, S gâtâya.

*Read at 2.5× and 4×.* °muttona *without a circumflex (2.5×; "°muttonâ cannot be excluded" at that zoom — flagged);* aṇaṇâ *with sub-dots under both* n*'s (2.5×; the first dot faint — flagged);* S anna. *plain.*

### English (Mrs Rhys Davids 1909, p. 10 [leaf 56, printed "10"])
> **II**  
> **Verse wherewith the Exalted One frequently exhorted Muttā while a Student.**
>
> Get free, Liberta,² free e’en as the Moon  
> From out the Dragon’s jaws³ sails clear on high.  
> Wipe off the debts that hinder thee,⁴ and so,  
> With heart at liberty, break thou thy fast.   (2)

*Read at 1× (verse) and 1.3× (notes). ² after the comma of* Liberta,*; ³ directly after* jaws*; ⁴ after the comma of* thee,*; curly apostrophes in* e’en *and* Dragon’s*; wide space before* (2)*. ## Verse 4 · Tisse sikkhassu sikkhâya … cara loke anâsavâ

> Tisse sikkhassu sikkhâya mâ taṃ yogâ upaccaguṃ |  
> sabbayogavisaṃyuttâ cara loke anâsavâ. || 4 ||  
> Tissâ. ||

*Read at 1.2× (lines 13–15 strips).* upaccaguṃ *final* ṃ*;* sabbayogavisaṃyuttâ *one word, with* ṃ *before* y *(the apparatus: P* °visayuttâ*);* anâsavâ *two circumflexes; attribution* Tissâ. || *indented as* Puṇṇâ. ||*.*

Pischel, apparatus to lines 13–15 (apparatus line 6): 13, S bhikkhassu.—14, P °visayuttâ.—15, *om. cdd.*

*Read at 2.5× and 4×.* bhikkhassu *with* a *(4×, not* bhikkhessu *as one 2.5× pass had it);* °visayuttâ *without the nasal (4×);* om. cdd. *in italics.*

### English (Mrs Rhys Davids 1909, pp. 12–13 [leaves 58–59])
> O Tissā ! train thyself in the trainings three.  
> See that the great conjuncture⁴ now at hand  
> Pass thee not by !  Unloose all other yokes,  
> And fare thou forth purged of the deadly
