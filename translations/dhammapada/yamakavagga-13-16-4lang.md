## Verse 13 · Yathā agāraṁ ducchannaṁ … rāgo samativijjhati

### Pāli (Fausbøll 1900, p. 4 [leaf 0021, printed "4"]; verse 13)
> Yathā agāraṁ¹ ducchannaṁ vuṭṭhi² samativijjhati  
> evaṁ abhāvitaṁ cittaṁ rāgo samativijjhati.

*No punctuation after* samativijjhati *in the first line (3×, 4× for v. 14's twin). The dot of* agāraṁ *and* ducchannaṁ *is read at 3×, 6× and 10× (both stand before a consonant);* vuṭṭhi *with two dotted* ṭ *(6×). The marginal figure* 13. *is heavily inked and at 8× looks almost like an* 8*; its left side is open and the sequence 13. 14. 15. 16. fixes it. The numerals ¹ and ² are read at 3×, 6× and (¹) 10×.*

Fausbøll, *Variants and Notes*, V. 13 (foot of leaf 0022, the Latin facing leaf, line 1 running into line 2): ¹ Cᵏ Cᶜ yathāgāraṁ. ² Sᵏ -ī.

*V. 13 opens on the first foot line of leaf 0022 immediately after V. 11's run-over* 339. Cfr. Divyāvadāna p. 384,5. *(2.5×, 8×; the small baseline 5 again at 14×, as the 9–12 file read it). The line ends after* yathāgāraṁ. *and the second note* ² Sᵏ -ī. *opens line 2 (2.5×, 8×, 14× — the ² is heavily inked, its counter filled, but has the curved head and flat foot of the 2s beside it, not the single stroke of the 1s). Above the hyphen of this first* -ī. *are two small slanted ticks (8×, 10×, 14×) — ink, not a diacritic, not reproduced. The sigla* Cᵏ *(no digit, 8×)*, Cᶜ *and* Sᵏ *are expanded in the 1–4 file's Conventions from Fausbøll's own Abbreviations;* Sᵏ *= the Siamese royal edition, here attesting* vuṭṭhī *with a long* ī.

### English (Müller 1881, SBE X, p. 6 [tif 0066, printed "6"]; verse 13)
> 13. As rain breaks through an ill-thatched house, passion will break through an unreflecting mind.

Müller, note 13 (p. 6): 13. See Beal, Dhammapada, p. 65.

*Read at 1.5× and 3×: no italics, one comma, the digits* 13 *and* 65*. No superscript reference numerals in vv. 12–15 on p. 6 (1.5×, checked line by line).*

## Verse 14 · Yathā agāraṁ succhannaṁ … rāgo na samativijjhati

### Pāli (Fausbøll 1900, p. 4; verse 14)
> Yathā agāraṁ¹ succhannaṁ vuṭṭhi² na samativijjhati  
> evaṁ subhāvitaṁ cittaṁ rāgo na samativijjhati.

*The first line runs to the right edge of the type area; the 3× crop clipped its last* i*, and a 4× crop of the right end reads* na samativijjhati *complete, with no punctuation after it. Under the* a *after the* m *of* samativijjhati *(line 1) is a speck (3×, 4×) — not a printed under-dot, not reproduced. The ¹ after* agāraṁ *is read at 3×, 6× and 10×; the ² after* vuṭṭhi *at 3× and 6×.*

Fausbøll, *Variants and Notes*, V. 14 (foot of leaf 0022, line 2): ² Sᵏ -ī.

**The text prints two numerals in v. 14 (¹ after* agāraṁ*, ² after* vuṭṭhi*), but the apparatus entry* V. 14. *carries only* ² Sᵏ -ī. *— there is no* ¹ *between* V. 14. *and the* ²*: read so at 2.5×, 8×, 14× (strip ending at the stop) and at a fresh 10× cut of the whole left half of the line, where the single numeral has the curved head and flat foot of a 2 and can be compared directly with the stroke-shaped ¹ after* V. 15. *on the same line. Presumably the* ¹ *of v. 14 is meant to repeat V. 13's* Cᵏ Cᶜ yathāgāraṁ *(the same word in the same position); that is Sarasvatī's inference, not something the leaf prints. Reproduced as printed, not corrected.*

## Taishō 562b05b–b07 · 鄙夫染人 … 行成潔芳 — two stanzas with no Pāli parallel in this chapter

### Chinese (T210, Taishō vol. 4 p. 562b05b–b07 [leaf 0561]) — no Pāli parallel in this chapter; identification ours, cf. the 1–4 file's ❽ 下二頌巴利文無
> `T04n0210_p0562b05` 〔淫泆不生 — end of the v. 14 stanza〕　⓳鄙夫染人　如近臭物  
> `T04n0210_p0562b06` 漸迷習非　不覺成惡　賢夫染人  
> `T04n0210_p0562b07` 如⓴近香熏　進智習善　行成潔芳

Kaeriten as read (3×): b05 染⁽レ⁾ · 如⁽レ⁾ 近⁽二⁾ 物⁽一⁾; b06 習⁽レ⁾ · 不⁽レ⁾ 成⁽レ⁾ · 染⁽レ⁾; b07 如⁽レ⁾ 近⁽二⁾ 熏⁽一⁾ · 習⁽レ⁾ · 成⁽二⁾ 芳⁽一⁾ — in b07 the ⓴ is set between 如 and 近, and 如's レ is displaced below the ring (3×). The ringed ⓳ stands before 鄙, the first character of the stanza. **Taishō foot note ⓳ (foot line 3; 2.5×, 6×, 8× across two overlapping segments, all seven characters read): 下二頌巴利文無** — "the two stanzas below have no Pāli" — the Taishō editors' own concordance note, of the same form as ❽ 下二頌巴利文無 on a17 in the 1–4 file; **the ROADMAP's "⑲" is found, and it says what was hoped.** Taishō foot note ⓴ (6×, 8×): 近＝附【三】 — 宋元明 read 附 for 近 (如附香熏). *On the two stanzas.* 鄙夫染人 如近臭物 漸迷習非 不覺成惡 ‖ 賢夫染人 如近香熏 進智習善 行成潔芳 — the base man stains others as nearness to a foul thing does: gradually deluded, practising wrong, unawares he becomes evil; the worthy man stains others as nearness to fragrant incense does: advancing in wisdom, practising good, his conduct becomes pure and fragrant.
