- This settles that T210 法句經 uses **OPEN-ONLY chapter transitions** — the new chapter's HEADING marks the boundary, and the preceding chapter has NO explicit close-colophon.
- A NEW cross-edition typographic sub-category distinct from Fausbøll's TWO-SIDED close+open convention (Pāli `Attavaggo dvādasamo.` + ch-13 heading on next leaf) and from Müller's TWO-SIDED chapter-ends-mid-page + fresh-recto-opens-new-chapter convention.

### Cross-language convergence on V. 166 attha

The Pāli V. 166 has three `attha`-root occurrences (`Attadatthaṁ` + `atthadatthaṁ` + `sadatthapasuto`). The T210 14th stanza L2 ends `如是得所欲` ("thus obtains what-is-desired"). The Chinese translator renders attha as `所欲` ("what is desired, one's desire-object") — a DESIRE-ORIENTED rendering distinct from Müller's DUTY-ORIENTED rendering. The samples' FIRST attested TRIPLE-CROSS-EDITION DIVERGENCE ON A SINGLE KEYWORD where Pāli attha = Müller duty = Chinese 所欲 desire. Settles that the Pāli term attha is SEMANTICALLY UNDERSPECIFIED and the three editions narrow it toward three different poles (object/purpose | duty/moral-good | desire/goal).

### 愛身品 14-vs-13 stanza count discrepancy (continued from 11-11)

The 愛身品 chapter heading declares `十有三章` (13 chapters/stanzas) but the chapter prints as 14 stanzas (12 four-char + 2 five-char per the 11-11 finding). Today's chapter-close pixel-attestation confirms the 14-stanza count with no correction to the heading. Settles the discrepancy as either (a) a Taishō editorial counting divergence from the declared heading (where the final two five-char stanzas count as ONE combined stanza of 8 pādas), or (b) a scribal/compositional inconsistency between the declared count and the actual printed stanzas. ⟨Sinologist flag⟩ for verification against CBETA TEI T04n0210.xml counting.
