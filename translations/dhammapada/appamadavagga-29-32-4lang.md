*On the parallel.* 不爲時自恣 能制漏得盡 自恣魔得便 如師子搏鹿 — one who does not indulge himself when the moment allows can master the outflows and bring them to an end; the self-indulgent gives Māra his chance, as a lion seizes a deer — answers v. 29 in sense, not pāda by pāda: *appamatto pamattesu* ≈ 不爲時自恣 against 自恣 (heedlessness as self-indulgence); *suttesu bahujāgaro* has no counterpart; *abalassaṁ va sīghasso hitvā yāti sumedhaso* — the racer leaving the hack — becomes the lion and the deer, 魔得便 (Māra's advantage) being the translator's own figure for what befalls the heedless, and 能制漏得盡 the attainment the Pāli leaves implicit. The identification is Sarasvatī's; the Taishō prints none.
