## Conventions

- **Fausbøll**: as in the earlier files (anusvāra **ṁ**; marginal figures; superscripts keyed to `V. 56.` … `V. 59.`; sigla **Bʳ Cᶜ Sᵏ Cᵏ**; `=` two bars; `cfr.`). New here: (a) **`y’ āyaṁ¹`** (56.1) — "y immediately followed by an apostrophe (no space between y and the apostrophe; the apostrophe at x-height against the y), then a space, then ā with a flat macron + yaṁ" (8×, 16×) — y + ’ + space + āyaṁ, not *yā*, not *yv-*; the apparatus **`¹ Cᶜ | prints yā ’yaṁ,`** — "yā WITH a clear macron; a word-space; the apostrophe immediately before the y of yaṁ, touching it, not attached to the preceding a" (16×) — and **`Bʳ Sᵏ have yvāyaṁ.`** — "the second letter a v (pointed bottom, no descender), ā macron, full stop" (8×, 16×): three positions of one elision (*yo ayaṁ*), the text's, Cᶜ's and the Burmese-Sinhalese *yvāyaṁ* — the dispatch's `ya ’yaṁ` (short a) wrong by the leaf, the OCR's `yvàyam` right in its letters, the glance's `yyāyaṁ` wrong; (b) **`tagaracandanī²`** — final ī with a macron, the ² raised; **at the baseline between the ī and the ² "a full point, a small squarish dot practically touching / merging with the right end of the ī's foot serif"** (24×; 16×: "a small dot-like mark at the baseline"; 8×: "nothing follows the ī but the ²") — 2 : 1 for a baseline dot; **carried without it** (no witness in the apparatus has a stop there, and `² Bʳ -danaṁ.` keys the word's ending) and **recorded as a dropped sort or speck touching the serif** — the Pāli reader's; (c) **`sampannasīlānaṁ`** (57.1) — the final m's dot **above** (8× both halves, 16×), with "a separate speck below the word, a small comma-like / diagonal mark under the a of naṁ" (16×) — the 3×'s "dot below the m" answered: a speck, not ṃ;
