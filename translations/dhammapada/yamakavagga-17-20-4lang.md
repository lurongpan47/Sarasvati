## Verse 17 · Idha tappati pecca tappati … bhiyyo tappati duggatiṁ gato

### Pāli (Fausbøll 1900, p. 4 [leaf 0021, printed "4"]; verse 17)
> Idha tappati pecca¹ tappati  
> pāpakārī ubhayattha tappati,  
> „pāpam² me katan“ ti tappati.  
> bhiyyo tappati duggatiṁ³ gato.

### English (Müller 1881, SBE X, p. 7 [tif 0067, printed "7"]; verse 17)
> 17. The evil-doer suffers in this world, and he suffers in the next ; he suffers in both. He suffers when he thinks of the evil he has done ; he suffers more when going on the evil path.

*Read at 1.5× and 2.5×: thin space before both semicolons, given as a space; a wide space after* both. *(2.5×);* evil-doer *with a real hyphen; no italics, no superscripts (both reads, line by line).*

Müller, note 17, 18 (p. 7, seven lines; one note for two verses, as `11, 12.` was): 17, 18. ' The evil path and the good path' are technical expressions for the descending and ascending scale of worlds through which all beings have to travel upward or downward, according to their deeds ; see Bigandet, Life of Gaudama, p. 5, note 4, and p. 449 ; Burnouf, Introduction, p. 599 ; Lotus, p. 865, l. 7 ; l. 11. Fausböll translates ' heaven and hell,' which comes to the same ; cf. vv. 126, 306.

*Read at 1.5× and 2.5×: no italics anywhere in the note; thin space after both opening quotation marks (2.5×) and before every semicolon; the closing marks set tight (*path' *,* hell,'*);* l. 7 ; l. 11. *with lower-case L; digits 5 · 4 · 449 · 599 · 865 · 126 · 306 at both zooms. The 13–16 file saw this note begin at 1.5× and did not carry it.*

### Chinese (T210 法句經 雙要品第九, Taishō vol. 4 p. 562b10c–b11 [leaf 0561]) — identification ours
> `T04n0210_p0562b10` 〔彼喜惟歡　見福心安 — end of the v. 16 stanza〕　今悔後悔  
> `T04n0210_p0562b11` 爲惡兩悔　厥爲自殃　受罪熱惱

Kaeriten as read (3×; b11's second pāda at 6×): b10 見⁽レ⁾ (今悔後悔 carries none); b11 爲⁽レ⁾ · 爲⁽二⁾ 殃⁽一⁾ · 受⁽レ⁾. No ringed numeral and no ＊ in b10 or b11 (3×). 厥 (not 其) and 爲 (not 為) as printed (3×, 6×). CBETA (finding aid) prints 厥, the same.

*On the parallel.* 今悔後悔 爲惡兩悔 厥爲自殃 受罪熱惱 — regret now, regret afterwards: doing evil, regret both ways; that becomes his own calamity, he receives the burning torment of his offence — answers v. 17 pāda by pāda (idha tappati pecca tappati ≈ 今悔後悔 / pāpakārī ubhayattha tappati ≈ 爲惡兩悔 / „pāpam me katan“ ti tappati ≈ 厥爲自殃 / bhiyyo tappati duggatiṁ gato ≈ 受罪熱惱), with 悔 for *tappati* where b08 had 憂 for *socati*, and 受罪熱惱 for the *duggati*. The identification is Sarasvatī's; the Taishō prints none.

## Verse 18 · Idha nandati pecca nandati … bhiyyo nandati suggatim gato

### Pāli (Fausbøll 1900, p. 6 [leaf 0023, printed "6"]; verse 18)
> Idha nandati pecca¹ nandati  
> katapuñño, ubhayattha nandati,  
> „puññam² me katan“ ti nandati,  
> bhiyyo nandati suggatim³ gato.

*The first verse of leaf 0023 (head: the figure* 6 *centred, no running head — 1.5×). Comma after* katapuñño *inside line 2 (3×; "a dot on the baseline with a tail" at 8×), comma at the end of line 2, and* **comma** *at the end of line 3 — the 1.5× orientation read gave a smudged period there, the 3× read a comma, and the 8× read a comma (dot with a short tail); given as a comma, unlike v. 17's period in the same place. The final* m *of* puññam *and of* suggatim *is plain (3×, 8×, 14× for the latter — "nothing above the m"). The numeral after* suggatim *is heavily inked: 3× read ³, 8× read it as "a thick 1 or blob", 14× read a 3 (two bulbs, a waist, open at the left, no stem or foot) — given as ³, which is also what the apparatus keys. A speck above the first* y *of* bhiyyo *(3×) is not a diacritic.*

Fausbøll, *Variants and Notes*, V. 18 (foot of leaf 0023, line 1 running into line 2): ¹ Bʳ pacca. ² Cᶜ Bʳ Sᵏ puññaṁ. ³ Bʳ -tañ, Sᵏ sugatiṁ. Suggatim metri causa.

*Line 1 of the five-line foot opens with* V. 18. *and ends at* sugatiṁ. *(2.5×, 8×, 12×); line 2 opens* Suggatim metri causa. *(the lemma letter-spaced on the leaf).* puññaṁ *dotted (8×, 12×) against the text's plain* puññam*;* -tañ, *with ñ (two legs, tilde — 8×, 12×, 16×) and a comma, not a period, before* Sᵏ*;* sugatiṁ *with one* g *and the dot (12×, 16×). The* m *of the letter-spaced* Suggatim *was read dotted at 2.5× and 8× and* **plain at 12× and 16×** *(the 16× cut set it directly against* sugatiṁ. *of line 1: "nothing above the m" / "a separate dot over the m") — given plain, as the text's* suggatim *is; the dissent is in the review section. A blot under the* B *of* Bʳ *in the third note (12×) and a speck after* causa. *(8×, 12×) are not characters.*
