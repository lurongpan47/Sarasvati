## Modern Chinese (现代汉语) — ⟨བརྟག⟩ Sarasvatī machine draft awaiting review

*The following is a Sarasvatī first-stakes modern-Chinese rendering of V. 167-170, built scan-first against the Fausbøll Pāli pixel-attested text. This is not a scholarly translation; it is a machine draft awaiting named-expert review. 卐 ⟨བརྟག⟩ = pending-review marker.*

```
⟨བརྟག⟩ 第十三  世俗品


167.  不要追随那卑劣的法，
      不要在懈怠中混度此生，
      不要信奉邪见，
      不要做这世俗的朋友。

168.  奋起吧！不要懈怠！
      依善法而行！
      行善法的人，此世与来世皆安乐而眠。

169.  当行善法，
      不要行恶法；
      行善法的人，此世与来世皆安乐而眠。

170.  视这世界如水泡，
      视这世界如阳焰——
      如此看待世间的人，
      死亡之王看不见他。
```

*⟨reader-flag⟩ V. 167 `lokavaddhano` rendering as "世俗的朋友" is taken from Müller's `a friend of the world` but may reflect Fausbøll's text rather than the MSS consensus `vaḍḍhano` (which would gloss as "worldly-increaser" / "one-who-promotes-the-world" rather than "friend"). V. 168 `na-ppamajjeyya` word-internal hyphen is not reflected in the modern Chinese. V. 170 `Maccurājā` rendered as "死亡之王" following Müller's `the king of death`; alternatively "死神" per common Buddhist Chinese usage — reader-flag for a Chinese Buddhist studies scholar on the Tang/Ming translation tradition preference.*
