Fausbøll, *Variants and Notes*, V. 5 (at the foot of the Latin p. 3, leaf 0020, first line, following the run-over *Bʳ veran.* of V. 4 n. ²): ¹ Cᵏ² Bʳ Sᵏ sammantīdha. Cfr. Mahābh. V, 2642.

### English (Müller 1881, SBE X, p. 5 [tif 0065]; verse 5)

No note on v. 5: the notes on p. 5 begin with 6.

**Cross-reference, not a new identification.** The stanza 慍於怨者 未甞無怨 不慍自除 是道可宗 (a19c–a20) is already given in the 1–4 file as the T210 counterpart of Dhp 3–4 *jointly*, with the remark that it "compresses 3–5ab". ## Verse 6 · Pare ca na vijānanti … tato sammanti medhagā

### Pāli (Fausbøll 1900, p. 2; verse 6)
> Pare ca na vijānanti: „mayam ettha yamāmase“,  
> ye ca tattha vijānanti tato sammanti medhagā.

No note numeral in v. 6 (checked at 2× and 4× on both lines). The colon after *vijānanti*, the „ “ marks and the comma after the closing “ are as printed; *mayam* is printed with a plain *m*.

Fausbøll, *Variants and Notes*, V. 6 (foot of leaf 0020, lines 2–4): V. 6. — Theragāthā p. 33 & 52. *Pare* = amamakā, Ctary. *Yamāmase*, cfr. Jāt. III ⁴⁸⁰⁄₁₀. I dare not deviate from the C.|tator's explanation.

*The key* V. 6. *is followed by a dash, not a numeral; the Jātaka reference is a stacked fraction (480 over 10, read at 5×, 8× and 12×); the break* C.|tator's *is printed with a baseline period after* C *(12×) — Fausbøll's abbreviation is* Ctator *(Commentator), so the period is a printer's break-mark or a damaged hyphen; reproduced as read.*

### English (Müller 1881, SBE X, p. 5; verse 6)
> 6. The world does not know that we must all come to an end here ;—but those who know it, their quarrels cease at once.

Müller, note 6 (p. 5): 6. Pare is explained by ' fools,' but it has that meaning by implication only. It is οἱ πόλλοι, cf. Vinaya, ed. Oldenberg, vol. i. p. 5, l. 4. Yamâmase, a 1 pers. plur. imp. Âtm., but really a Le*t* in Pâli. See Fausböll, Five *G*âtakas, p. 38.

*The Greek is printed with the acute on the first omicron (2×, 6×); "a 1 pers." has no superscript* st *(2×); the italic* t *of* Le*t* *and the italic* G *of* Five *G*âtakas *are his transliteration italics (ṭ, j).*

### Chinese (T210, Taishō vol. 4 p. 562a21–a22a) — identification ours
> `T04n0210_p0562a21` 不好責彼　務自省身　如有知此  
> `T04n0210_p0562a22` 永滅無患　〔行見⓬身淨　不攝諸根 — next stanza, see v. 7〕

Kaeriten as read (3×): a21 不⁽二⁾ 責⁽一レ⁾ 省⁽レ⁾ 如⁽レ⁾ 有⁽レ⁾ 知⁽レ⁾; a22 無⁽レ⁾ (for 永滅無患; the rest of a22 under v. 7). No note falls in a21 or in 永滅無患.

*On the parallel.* The stanza — do not delight in blaming others; attend to examining yourself; one who knows this has troubles extinguished for ever — is the stanza that follows the 慍於怨者 stanza and precedes the 行見身淨 ‖ 觀身不淨 pair (Dhp 7–8, below). Its position and its second half (如有知此 永滅無患 ≈ *ye ca tattha vijānanti tato sammanti medhagā*) make it the T210 counterpart of v. 6; its first half (不好責彼 務自省身) has no Pāli in v. 6 and reads like a moral expansion. The identification is Sarasvatī's; the Taishō prints none. The 1–4 file already excluded this stanza from v. 4 ("the beginning of the next thought (Dhp 5–6 range)"), which is consistent with placing it here.

## Verse 7 · Subhānupassiṁ viharantaṁ … vāto rukkhaṁ va dubbalaṁ

### Pāli (Fausbøll 1900, p. 2; verse 7)
> Subhānupassiṁ¹ viharantaṁ indriyesu asaṁvutaṁ  
> bhojanamhi câmattaññuṁ⁷ kusītaṁ hīnavīriyaṁ  
> taṁ ve pasahatī³ Māro vāto rukkhaṁ va dubbalaṁ.

*The numeral after* câmattaññuṁ *is printed in the shape of a* **7** *(three reads, at 2×, 4× and 8×: flat top bar with a small left serif, one straight unfooted diagonal, no curved head and no base line); the foot apparatus keys this note as* **²** *and the verse's other numerals are ¹ and ³. It is given here as read, ⁷, and flagged: a wrong-font sort or a damaged 2 — the reader should take it as note ².*

Fausbøll, *Variants and Notes*, V. 7 (foot of leaf 0020, lines 4–7): ¹ so Sᵏ, Bʳ -passi, Cᶜ -passī for -passiṁ? cfr. Ctary and v. 328; perhaps we are to read subhānupass’ viharantaṁ, cfr. v. 27. ² Cᵏ ca amattaññuṁ, Bʳ Sᵏ amattaññuṁ, omitting ca, cfr. v. 11. ³ Bʳ Sᵏ Cᵏ -ti. *Māra*, cfr. vv. 8. 34. 37. 40. 46. 57. 105. 175. 274. 337. 350.

*Resolved line-end hyphens:* -pas-|siṁ, subhānu-|pass’, amat-|taññuṁ. *The apostrophe of* pass’ *is comma-shaped at 12×; at 5× the word was first read* passiṁ *(wrongly — there is no* i *and no dotted* m*). The sigla* Cᵏ *in n. ² and n. ³ carry no digit (8×). Between* Cᶜ *and* -passī *stands a thin raised vertical tick lighter than the type (8×, 12×), read as an accident and not reproduced. The verse list is set with wide spacing across the whole measure and ends with a period after 350 (16× thresholded; a 10× read had commas after 57, 175 and 350 — not supported at 16×: every mark is a round dot on the baseline).*

### English (Müller 1881, SBE X, p. 5; verse 7)
> 7. He who lives looking for pleasures only, his senses uncontrolled, immoderate in his food, idle, and weak, Mâra (the tempter) will certainly overthrow him, as the wind throws down a weak tree.

Müller, note 7 (p. 5, two paragraphs): 7. Mâra must be taken in the Buddhist sense of ' tempter,' or ' evil spirit.' See Burnouf, Introduction, p. 76 : ' Mâra est le démon de l'amour, du péché et de la mort ; c'est le tentateur et l'ennemi de Buddha.' As to the definite meaning of vîrya, see Burnouf, Lotus, p. 548.
In the Buddhistical Sanskrit, kusîda, ' idle,' is the exact counterpart of the Pâli kusîta ; see Burnouf, Lotus, p. 548. On the change of Sanskrit d into Pâli t, see Kuhn, Beiträge zur Pali Grammatik, p. 40 ; Weber, Ind. Studien, XIII, p. 135.

## Verse 8 · Asubhānupassiṁ viharantaṁ … vāto selaṁ va pabbataṁ

### Pāli (Fausbøll 1900, p. 4 [leaf 0021, printed "4"]; verse 8)
> Asubhānupassiṁ¹ viharantaṁ indriyesu susaṁvutaṁ  
> bhojanamhi ca mattaññuṁ saddhaṁ āraddhavīriyaṁ  
> taṁ [ve]² na-ppasahatī Māro vāto selaṁ va pabbataṁ.

*The square brackets round* ve *are true brackets (4×, 8×), the numeral ² stands after the closing bracket, the hyphen in* na-ppasahatī *is printed, and the final* ī *of* -pasahatī *carries a macron as in v. 7. In v. 8 Fausbøll prints* ca mattaññuṁ *as two words (no circumflex) against v. 7's* câmattaññuṁ *— both as printed.*

Fausbøll, *Variants and Notes*, V. 8 (foot of leaf 0021, first line): ¹ read -pass’? ² taṁ ve, sq. all the MSS. & Editions.

*The mark after* -pass *is a small raised dot at 14× and 16× (grey and thresholded) rather than a comma-shaped apostrophe; it is given as the apostrophe his V. 7 note uses for the same elision (*subhānupass’*), and flagged. The abbreviation is* sq. *(not* seq. *or* sqq.*), read at 10×.* Editions. *with a final period was read at 1× and 8×; the 10× strip stopped at* MSS. *— the close of the note is therefore on two reads, not three.*

### English (Müller 1881, SBE X, p. 5; verse 8)
> 8. He who lives without looking for pleasures, his senses well controlled, moderate in his food, faithful and strong, him Mâra will certainly not overthrow, any more than the wind throws down a rocky mountain.

No note on v. 8: Müller's notes on p. 5 run 6, 7, 9; note 9 (the Kâsâva / Kâshâya pun) begins on p. 5 and runs over onto p. 6, whose foot then carries 10, 11–12, 13, 15 — nothing keyed to 5–8 spills onto p. 6 (tif 0066 read today).

### Chinese (T210, Taishō vol. 4 p. 562a24b–a26a) — identification ours
> `T04n0210_p0562a24` 〔如風靡草 — end of the v. 7 stanza〕　觀身不淨　能攝諸根  
> `T04n0210_p0562a25` 食知節度　常樂精進　不爲邪動  
> `T04n0210_p0562a26` 如風大山　〔不吐毒態　欲心馳騁 — next stanza, not claimed〕

Kaeriten as read (3×; 6× for a26a): a24 觀⁽二⁾ 淨⁽一⁾ 攝⁽二⁾ 根⁽一⁾; a25 知⁽二⁾ 度⁽一⁾ 不⁽二⁾ 爲⁽レ⁾ 動⁽一⁾; a26 如⁽レ⁾ 風⁽二⁾ 山⁽一⁾ (and, for the next stanza's first pāda, 不⁽レ⁾ 吐⁽二⁾ 態⁽一⁾). No ringed note falls in a24b–a26a; the next note on the page is ⓯ (戒＝我【聖】), further down the register.

*On the parallel.* 觀身不淨 能攝諸根 食知節度 常樂精進 不爲邪動 如風大山 — contemplating the body as impure, able to restrain the senses, knowing measure in food, ever delighting in effort, not moved by the evil one as wind cannot move a great mountain — answers v. 8 pāda by pāda (asubhānupassiṁ / indriyesu susaṁvutaṁ / bhojanamhi ca mattaññuṁ / saddhaṁ āraddhavīriyaṁ / na-ppasahatī Māro / vāto selaṁ va pabbataṁ), with 常樂精進 standing where the Pāli has *saddhaṁ āraddhavīriyaṁ* (faith is not rendered). The identification is Sarasvatī's; the Taishō prints none.
