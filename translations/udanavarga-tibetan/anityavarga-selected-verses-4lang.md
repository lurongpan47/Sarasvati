## ⚠️ Erratum · 2026-09-18 (read this first)

This file replaces `anityavarga-verses1-4-4lang.md` (committed 2026-09-17). That first version was checked on 2026-09-18 against the actual scan of Rockhill 1892 (Internet Archive, `udnavargacolle00bkah`) and was found to contain **three false provenance claims**. They are corrected here and recorded so the error is auditable, not hidden:

1. **Verse numbering was wrong.** The four teachings are not Rockhill's chapter I verses 1–4. In Rockhill's edition, I.1–2 are the compiler's introductory verses; the *aniccā vata saṅkhārā* stanza is **I.3** (p. 2), the young-and-old / ripe-fruit stanza is **I.10** (p. 3), the potter's-vessels stanza is **I.12** (p. 3), and the cowherd stanza is **I.17** (p. 4, Rockhill's bracket "(135)" = Dhp 135).
2. **The English column was not verbatim Rockhill.** It was a paraphrase presented as "verbatim". The English below is now copied from the scan, with Rockhill's punctuation and parenthetical glosses preserved.
3. **Rockhill 1892 prints no Tibetan text.** The claim that the dbu-can column was "as printed in Rockhill 1892 pp. 1–2" was untrue. Rockhill's volume is an English translation with notes; it gives only occasional Tibetan words in romanization. The Tibetan column is therefore **not attested from a print source in this file**. It is a Sarasvatī draft of the Kangyur wording and is flagged `⟨བརྟག⟩` on every stanza, pending collation against a public-domain Tibetan edition — **Hermann Beckh, *Udānavarga: eine Sammlung buddhistischer Sprüche in tibetischer Sprache*, Berlin 1911** (Beckh d. 1937; PD) — or a Kangyur witness (Derge Tōh. 326, mdo sde *sa*).

Also corrected: the catalogue number (Tōh. 4099 is Prajñāvarman's commentary in the Tengyur, not the Kangyur root text); the Chinese parallel for I.3 (the famous 諸行無常，是生滅法… wording belongs to the *Mahāparinirvāṇa-sūtra*, not T210); the Pāli parallel citations (Sn 576–578 for the ripe-fruit / potter / young-and-old stanzas, per Rockhill's own footnotes to *Sallasutta* 3–5).

**What the file now honestly is:** an attested public-domain **English** rendering (Rockhill 1892) of four Tibetan Udānavarga stanzas, with Pāli and Chinese parallels from public-domain canonical texts, and a **draft** Tibetan source column awaiting collation. It remains the Tibetan branch's first dedicated sample, but the source-language column is a `⟨བརྟག⟩` draft, not attested text.

## About this file

**The Udānavarga** (Skt.; Tib. *Ched-du brjod-pa'i tshoms* ཆེད་དུ་བརྗོད་པའི་ཚོམས) is the Sarvāstivāda / Mūlasarvāstivāda counterpart to the Pāli Dhammapada, attributed to the compiler **Dharmatrāta** (Tib. *Chos-skyob* ཆོས་སྐྱོབ). It is preserved complete in Tibetan (Kangyur, mdo-sde, Tōh. 326) and partially or in adaptation in Chinese (T210, T211, T212, T213), with a Sanskrit text recovered from Turfan manuscripts. The Tibetan version has 33 chapters. Chapter 1 — **Anityavarga / mi rtag pa'i tshoms** — treats impermanence and opens the collection.

**Columns**:
- **Tibetan (dbu-can) ⟨བརྟག⟩** — Sarasvatī draft of the Kangyur wording. **Not transcribed from a print source.** Verse I.3 follows the standard Kangyur wording that recurs across Vinaya and sūtra texts and is stable across witnesses; I.10, I.12, I.17 are lower-confidence drafts. Every stanza must be collated against Beckh 1911 or a Kangyur witness before the flag can be removed.
- **Wylie transliteration** — mechanical romanization (Turrell Wylie 1959) of the draft Tibetan; inherits the draft's uncertainty.
- **English (Rockhill 1892)** — W. Woodville Rockhill, *Udânavarga: A Collection of Verses from the Buddhist Canon compiled by Dharmatrâta, being the Northern Buddhist Version of Dhammapada, translated from the Tibetan of the Bkah-hgyur, with notes and extracts from the commentary of Pradjnâvarman* (London: Trübner & Co., 1892), Book I "Impermanency", pp. 2–4. **Verbatim from the scan**, including Rockhill's parenthetical glosses. Public domain worldwide (pre-1929 US; author d. 1914).
- **Pāli parallel** — Sutta-nipāta *Sallasutta* (Sn 3.8) and Dhammapada, PTS text (public domain). Verse numbers follow the common continuous Sn numbering; Rockhill's footnotes cite the same stanzas by their within-sutta number (*Sallasutta* 3, 4, 5).
- **Chinese parallel** — T210 《法句經》 (attrib. Zhi Qian et al., 3rd c. CE; public domain by age; wording via CBETA public-facing corpus), or another PD canonical text where indicated. Wording flagged `⟨བརྟག⟩` where a Sinologist should confirm the exact CBETA reading.

**Status**: The English column is attested public-domain text. The Pāli and Chinese columns are public-domain canonical text (not Sarasvatī drafts) but their exact readings need confirmation by a named reviewer. The Tibetan and Wylie columns are Sarasvatī drafts.

## Rockhill I.3 · "Alas! the impermanency of created things"

### Tibetan ⟨བརྟག⟩ (standard Kangyur wording; collate against Beckh 1911 / Derge Tōh. 326)
> ཀྱེ་མ་འདུ་བྱེད་མི་རྟག་སྟེ། །
> སྐྱེ་ཞིང་འཇིག་པའི་ཆོས་ཅན་ཡིན། །
> སྐྱེས་ནས་འཇིག་པར་འགྱུར་བ་སྟེ། །
> དེ་དག་ཉེ་བར་ཞི་བ་བདེ། །

### Wylie transliteration
> kye ma 'du byed mi rtag ste /
> skye zhing 'jig pa'i chos can yin /
> skyes nas 'jig par 'gyur ba ste /
> de dag nye bar zhi ba bde //

### English (Rockhill 1892, p. 2, verse 3, verbatim)
> Alas! the impermanency of created things (samkara); what is created is subject to decay. As what has been born must come to destruction, happy they who are at rest!

*Rockhill's footnote 1 to this verse: "Comp. Beal, Dhammapada, p. 32; Rhys-Davids, Parinibbanasutta, p. 117; Anityata Sutra (Mdo xxvi.), fol. 246a."*

### Pāli parallel (DN 16.6.10, Sakka's verse at the Parinibbāna; also SN 6.15, SN 15.20)
> Aniccā vata saṅkhārā, uppādavayadhammino;
> uppajjitvā nirujjhanti, tesaṃ vūpasamo sukho.

### Chinese parallels
> **T210 《法句經·無常品》** (3rd c. CE, PD): 所行非常，謂興衰法，夫生輙死，此滅為樂。⟨བརྟག⟩
>
> **Cf. *Mahāparinirvāṇa-sūtra* 《大般涅槃經》 (T374/T375, Dharmakṣema, 5th c., PD)** — the wording most Chinese readers know: 諸行無常，是生滅法，生滅滅已，寂滅為樂。(Not T210; corrected 2026-09-18.)

## Rockhill I.10 · "Some are old, and some are young"

### Tibetan ⟨བརྟག⟩ (Sarasvatī draft, low confidence — collate before any use)
> ན་ཞིང་གཞོན་དང་བྱིས་པ་དང་། །
> བུད་མེད་སྐྱེས་པ་མང་པོ་ནི། །
> མི་རྟག་པས་བརླག་པར་བྱེད་དེ། །
> ཤིང་གི་འབྲས་བུ་སྨིན་པ་བཞིན། །

### Wylie transliteration
> na zhing gzhon dang byis pa dang /
> bud med skyes pa mang po ni /
> mi rtag pas brlag par byed de /
> shing gi 'bras bu smin pa bzhin //

### English (Rockhill 1892, p. 3, verse 10, verbatim)
> Some are old, and some are young, some are grown up; by degrees they all do disappear, like ripe fruit falling.

*Rockhill's footnote: "Comp. Sallasutta (Sutta Nipata), 5."*

### Pāli parallel (Sn 578, *Sallasutta* 5; ripe-fruit image at Sn 576, *Sallasutta* 3)
> Daharā ca mahantā ca, ye bālā ye ca paṇḍitā;
> sabbe maccuvasaṃ yanti, sabbe maccuparāyanā.
>
> Phalānamiva pakkānaṃ, pāto papatanā bhayaṃ;
> evaṃ jātāna maccānaṃ, niccaṃ maraṇato bhayaṃ.

### Chinese parallel
> — (no T210 parallel located with confidence; T212 《出曜經·無常品》 is the fuller Chinese Udānavarga and should carry it — reviewer to supply.) ⟨བརྟག⟩

## Rockhill I.12 · "As with the shining vases made from clay by the potter"

### Tibetan ⟨བརྟག⟩ (Sarasvatī draft, low confidence — collate before any use)
> ཇི་ལྟར་རྫ་མཁན་གྱི་སྣོད་རྣམས། །
> ཆུང་དུ་ཆེ་བ་ཐམས་ཅད་དུ། །
> མཐའ་ནི་ཆག་པ་ཡིན་པ་ལྟར། །
> དེ་བཞིན་སྐྱེ་བོའི་ཚེ་ཡང་ནོ། །

### Wylie transliteration
> ji ltar rdza mkhan gyi snod rnams /
> chung du che ba thams cad du /
> mtha' ni chag pa yin pa ltar /
> de bzhin skye bo'i tshe yang no //

### English (Rockhill 1892, p. 3, verse 12, verbatim)
> It is with the life of mortal man as with the shining vases made from clay by the potter—they all finish by being destroyed.

*Rockhill's footnote: "Comp. Burnouf, Lotus de la Bonne Loi, p. 86; and Sallasutta (Sutta Nipata), 4."*

### Pāli parallel (Sn 577, *Sallasutta* 4)
> Yathāpi kumbhakārassa, katā mattikabhājanā;
> sabbe bhedanapariyantā, evaṃ maccāna jīvitaṃ.

### Chinese parallel (T210 《法句經·無常品》)
> 譬如陶家，埏埴作器，一切要壞，人命亦然。⟨བརྟག⟩

## Rockhill I.17 (Dhp 135) · "As a cowherd with his staff"

### Tibetan ⟨བརྟག⟩ (Sarasvatī draft, low confidence — collate before any use)
> ཇི་ལྟར་གནག་རྫིས་བེར་ཀའི་དབྱུག་པས། །
> ཕྱུགས་རྣམས་གནས་སུ་བརྡ་ལ་བཞིན། །
> དེ་བཞིན་ན་བ་འཆི་བ་དག །
> སྐྱེ་བོའི་ཚེ་ནི་བསྒྲོན་པར་བྱེད། །

### Wylie transliteration
> ji ltar gnag rdzis ber ka'i dbyug pas /
> phyugs rnams gnas su brda la bzhin /
> de bzhin na ba 'chi ba dag /
> skye bo'i tshe ni bsgron par byed //

### English (Rockhill 1892, p. 4, verse 17, verbatim)
> As a cowherd with his staff gathers his cattle into the stable, so disease and old age bring mankind to the lord of death.

*Rockhill's bracket "(135)" marks the Dhammapada parallel.*

### Pāli parallel (Dhp 135)
> Yathā daṇḍena gopālo, gāvo pājeti gocaraṃ;
> evaṃ jarā ca maccu ca, āyuṃ pājenti pāṇinaṃ.

### Chinese parallel (T210 《法句經·刀杖品》, the Daṇḍavagga counterpart)
> 譬人操杖，行牧食牛，老死猶然，亦養命去。⟨བརྟག⟩
