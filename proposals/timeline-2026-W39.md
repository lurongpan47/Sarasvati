### evt_086 · 西藏梵文贝叶经系列校订本（STTAR，2004年至今）
- **tradition:** `sanskrit`
- **period_zh:** 2004年至今
- **period_en:** 2004–present
- **title_zh:** 中国藏学研究中心×奥地利科学院 · 西藏自治区梵文文本系列（STTAR）28 卷
- **title_script:** Sanskrit Texts from the Tibetan Autonomous Region
- **title_en:** STTAR — critical editions of Tibet's Sanskrit palm-leaf codices (28 vols)
- **Why:** Closes the gap after evt_066 (Sāṅkṛtyāyana photographs, 1934–38) and evt_043 (codices survive in Tibet). STTAR is the modern critical-edition phase of exactly those Potala/Norbulingka manuscripts (Pramāṇaviniścaya, Pañcaskandhakavibhāṣā, Pañcaviṃśatisāhasrikā, etc.), published Beijing–Vienna since 2005.
- **Evidence:** 中新社 2026-09-20 (第八届全国藏文古籍文献整理与研究高层论坛): CTRC–ÖAW cooperation agreement 2004; "西藏自治区梵文文本系列丛书" now 28 vols. Corroborated: ÖAW IKGA series page (vol. 7 2010, vol. 20 2016/17, vol. 21 2020).
- **Links:** https://news.qq.com/rain/a/20260920A0988000 · https://www.oeaw.ac.at/en/ikga/publications/series/sanskrit-texts-from-the-tibetan-autonomous-region-sttar · https://networks.h-net.org/node/6060/discussions/163795/new-book-sanskrit-texts-tibetan-autonomous-region-volume-20

### evt_087 · 泰国御制英译三藏（45 卷，2019年至今）
- **tradition:** `seasia`
- **period_zh:** 2019年至今
- **period_en:** 2019–present
- **title_zh:** 泰国僧王委员会御制英译三藏 · 45 卷 21,941 页（律 8 · 经 25 · 论 12）
- **title_script:** พระไตรปิฎกฉบับภาษาอังกฤษ
- **title_en:** Thailand's royal English Tipiṭaka — 45 volumes from the Syāmaraṭṭha edition
- **Why:** Direct descendant of evt_067 (Chulalongkorn typeset Tipiṭaka 1893): a state-sponsored full English rendering of the Thai-script Pāli canon, launched by the Sangha Supreme Council for Rama X's coronation (2019). First complete government-backed English Tipiṭaka from a Theravāda state.
- **Evidence:** Thai PRD 2019-06-12 (project launch, Sangha Supreme Council committee); Khaosod English 2026-09-12 / SuttaCentral Discourse 2026-09-13 (first 2026 patronage-committee meeting at Government House, 11 Sept 2026: 45 vols / 21,941 pp; three sub-committees formed; print + e-book planned).
- **Links:** https://thailand.prd.go.th/mobile_detail.php?cid=4&nid=8085 · https://www.khaosodenglish.com/featured/2026/09/12/thailand-moves-ahead-with-45-volume-english-tipitaka-project/ · https://discourse.suttacentral.net/t/45225
