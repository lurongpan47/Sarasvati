# India · Week 3 proposal — Rummindeī (Lumbinī) pillar-inscription

**Date:** 2026-11-16 (PST), branch cycle: 7-branch rotation second pass, India week 3 after REXII (09-13) · REXIII (09-15) · Calcutta-Bairāṭ (09-28).

## Candidate A (preferred — to be built this session)

**Rummindeī Pillar-Inscription** — Aśoka's Lumbinī edict, c. 249 BCE. Hultzsch 1925 *Corpus Inscriptionum Indicarum* vol. I (new edition) pp. 164–165, plate facing p. 164 (leaf n339 of IA `InscriptionsOfAsoka.NewEditionByE.Hultzsch`). Five Brāhmī lines on the pillar Aśoka erected at the Buddha's birthplace during his 20th regnal year (**atana āgācha mahīyite hida Budhe jāte Sakyamuni ti**). The village of Lumbinī is made tax-free and granted a reduced one-eighth share. The **only** epigraphic witness to a named monarch marking the birthplace of the Buddha, and (with the Nigālī Sāgar) one of only two Aśokan "pilgrim's" inscriptions. Text 5 lines + translation A–C + 3 footnotes — compact enough for one scan-first build inside today's window.

**Why valuable:** (i) Bridges India branch to the biographical-Buddha tradition that all 8 branches depend on; (ii) corroborates the Lumbinī locus independently of the Mahāparinibbāna-sutta's Jātānussati list; (iii) a short enough text for a careful Prakrit + English + 现代汉语 + བོད་ཡིག reading with full footnote apparatus. Targets: fills the 20-years-after-consecration gap in Pan's Prime Directive archive between REXII/XIII (yr 12-13) and the Sarnath Schism Edict (yr 27-ish).

**Why public domain:** Hultzsch (†1927) + Dover 1925 imprint, worldwide PD since 1998; IA PDF at `InscriptionsOfAsoka.NewEditionByE.Hultzsch` openly distributed. Prakrit text itself anonymous & ~2,250 years old; English translation Hultzsch's own.

**Resource estimate:** ~20 image calls, 1 session; builds `translations/ashoka-edicts/rummindei-pillar-inscription-4lang.md` scan-first. Follow the existing `calcutta-bairat-rock-inscription-4lang.md` template.
