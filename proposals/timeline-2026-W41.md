### evt_088 · 犍陀罗桦皮写卷入藏伊斯兰堡博物馆（2022年至今）
- **tradition:** `silkroad`
- **period_zh:** 2022年至今
- **period_en:** 2022–present
- **title_zh:** 犍陀罗语佉卢文桦皮写卷捐赠伊斯兰堡博物馆 · 50–60 卷，现存最大犍陀罗写本群，悉尼大学 GMP 在地保护与刊布
- **title_script:** Islamabad Museum Collection · Gāndhārī / Kharoṣṭhī
- **title_en:** Islamabad Museum Gāndhārī scrolls — largest known collection (50–60), conserved and edited in Pakistan
- **Why:** Continues evt_010 (1st–2nd c. Gāndhārī birch-bark, the oldest Buddhist manuscripts) into the present: the silkroad lane had no row after the 12th century. Contents include the Aṭṭhakavagga, saṅghādisesa Prātimokṣa rules, Samādhirāja and Pratyutpanna sūtras — the earliest Indic witnesses of those texts. The collection stayed in its region of origin, reversing the export pattern of the British Library / Senior / Schøyen finds.
- **Evidence:** University of Sydney GMP project page (donation 26 Dec 2022 with Khyentse Foundation support; DOAM–Sydney agreement signed 20 Dec 2022 after Pakistan Cabinet approval 2 Dec 2022; ≥50–60 scrolls; radiocarbon 1st c. BCE–2nd c. CE); Buddhistdoor Global 2023-03-15 (donation) and 2025-12-29 (Khyentse Foundation extends conservation support); Khyentse Foundation 2025-12-22 update (conservation lab at the museum, scans, radiocarbon, Dec 2024 Taxila workshop); H-Net project notice.
- **Links:** https://gandhari-texts.sydney.edu.au/gandhari-manuscripts/ · https://www.buddhistdoor.net/news/khyentse-foundation-supports-donation-of-ancient-gandhari-buddhist-manuscripts-to-pakistan/ · https://www.buddhistdoor.net/news/buddhist-heritage-khyentse-foundation-to-extend-support-for-conservation-of-gandhari-scrolls-at-islamabad-museum/ · https://khyentsefoundation.org/story/revealing-hidden-treasures/

### evt_089 · 「圆满法藏」鸠摩罗什计划 · 藏文大藏经汉译（2019年至今）
- **tradition:** `tibetan`
- **period_zh:** 2019年至今
- **period_en:** 2019–present
- **title_zh:** 钦哲基金会「圆满法藏·佛典汉译」鸠摩罗什计划 · 汉译藏文甘珠尔/丹珠尔中汉藏所缺经论，六十年规划，2021 年首部上线
- **title_script:** 圓滿法藏·佛典漢譯
- **title_en:** Kumarajiva Project — Tibetan Kangyur/Tengyur texts absent from the Chinese canon rendered into Chinese (60-year plan)
- **Why:** Sister project of evt_083 (84000, Tibetan → English); the first organised programme to move the Tibetan canon into Chinese since the Qing. Lane = `tibetan` by the evt_083 / evt_087 convention (the canon being rendered, not the target language). ~65 % of the Mahāyāna śāstras in the Tengyur have no Chinese version — this is the gap the project names.
- **Evidence:** Buddhistdoor Tea House 2019-07-12 (launch, 60-year horizon); Buddhistdoor Global 2021-07-07 (first text, *Brahmaparipṛcchā*, online Feb 2021; 7 texts after two years) and 2024-09-23 (focus shifted to translator training); Khyentse Foundation project page; ymfz.org (institutional, Chinese name 圓滿法藏·佛典漢譯).
- **Links:** https://teahouse.buddhistdoor.net/kumarajiva-project-a-60-year-tibetan-chinese-translation-initiative-from-khyentse-foundation/ · https://www.buddhistdoor.net/news/khyentse-foundations-kumarajiva-project-marks-2nd-year-with-7-buddhist-texts-translated-into-chinese/ · https://www.buddhistdoor.net/news/khyentse-foundations-kumarajiva-project-shifts-focus-to-training-translators/ · https://khyentsefoundation.org/kf-projects/kumarajiva-project/ · https://www.ymfz.org/?lang=en
