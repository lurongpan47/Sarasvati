## Candidates

### A. **Therīgāthā, Ekakanipāta 1–5** *(preferred — built this session)*
- **What**: the first five single-verse psalms of the nuns — the anonymous *therikā* ("Sleep softly, little Sturdy"), Muttā, Puṇṇā, Tissā, "another Tissā" — with the two prose frames (*itthaṃ sudaṃ …*) that the Pāli text itself carries after vv. 1–2. Ten verse lines, five attribution lines, one page of Pischel's apparatus.
- **Pāli**: H. Oldenberg & R. Pischel, *The Thera- and Therî-gâthâ*, PTS / Henry Frowde, London 1883, **p. 123** (Therīgāthā text begins; 18 numbered lines + apparatus keyed by line number; sigla L P S B C from Pischel's Preface p. [121]).
- **English (PD)**: Mrs C. A. F. Rhys Davids, *Psalms of the Early Buddhists I. Psalms of the Sisters*, PTS / Frowde 1909, **pp. 9–13**, verses (1)–(5) with the notes keyed to the verses (p. 10 nn. 2–4, p. 12 nn. 1–2 and 4 → p. 13, p. 13 n. 1).
- **Scans (opened this session)**: IA `in.ernet.dli.2015.264415` leaf 137 (= p. 123, 3643 × 4995) and IA `psalmsofearlybud01davi` leaves 55–59 (= pp. 9–13, ~2400 × 4050), IIIF full size; page numbers read from the leaves.
- **Targets**: 现代汉语 + བོད་ཡིག drafts ⟨བརྟག⟩. No Chinese or Tibetan canonical Therīgāthā exists; the modern Chinese renderings (元亨寺 1990s, 鄧殿臣 1997) are in copyright.
