- **Why:** Settles a decades-long codicological reconstruction of the Cave 17 (藏经洞) Buddhist / Daoist / secular corpus — the single largest systematic effort to physically re-unite the diasporic Dunhuang manuscript fragments scattered across 6+ institutions on 3 continents (NLC Beijing, Tianjin, Zhejiang Museum, British Library, BnF, IOM St Petersburg). Project proposes new theory of 藏经洞 sealing / discard, revising long-standing hypotheses from Fujieda Akira and Rong Xinjiang. Parallels evt_013 (Dunhuang discovery 1900), evt_063 (IDP digitisation), evt_092 (NMK Korean-held Dunhuang scrolls). Direct Buddhist-canon-transmission event (大藏经南北系统 debate explicitly cited; 《金剛經》《法華經》《華嚴經》《無量壽經》 等 Mahāyāna sūtra fragments involved).
