# Southeast Asian Theravāda (`seasia`) · Weekly candidate proposal · 2026-09-21

**Branch selected**: `seasia` — the sixth of the seven non-Tibetan branches to receive a weekly proposal (Pāli 08-29 · Chinese 08-29 · Sanskrit 08-31 · Sinosphere 09-07 · Silk Road 09-14). Only `india` remains after this. The existing v0.9 asset (Karaṇīya Mettā Sutta) is a canonical text *chanted* in Southeast Asia; none of the archive yet contains a text *composed* there. This proposal fills that gap with the Burmese Pāli **nīti** literature, which VRI's Chaṭṭha Saṅgāyana edition carries in its `Añña › Nīti-Gantha-Saṅgaho` section.

## Candidates

### A. **Lokanīti, ch. 1 *Paṇḍitakaṇḍa*** *(preferred)*
- **Pāli**: VRI CST `cscd/e1005n.nrf0–6.xml` (7 kaṇḍas, 167 gāthās, ~17 k chars total; ch. 1 ≈ 40 gāthās, 4 k chars). Text machine-readable, verified by fetch today.
- **English (PD)**: James Gray, *Ancient Proverbs and Maxims from Burmese Sources; or, the Nīti Literature of Burma*, London 1886 — IA `cu31924052490913` (also `ancientproverbs00graygoog`). Gray's own note: Lokanīti is "the most popular in Burma … a text-book for the vernacular schools"; Burmese nissaya rendered 1835 under Bagyidaw.
- **Manuscript evidence**: BDRC Fragile Palm Leaves bundles W1FPL5982 / W1FPL1317 / W1FPL5721 contain Burmese-script Lokanīti — genuine SEAsia transmission.
- **Reading page**: Pāli (VRI) / English (Gray 1886, verbatim) / 漢文 draft / Tibetan draft / Burmese-script Pāli transliteration. Targets: **Chinese and Tibetan** — no rendering in either language is known.

### B. **Dhammanīti**, selected gāthās
- Pāli: VRI CST `cscd/e1003n.nrf.xml`; English: Gray 1886 pt. II. Larger (~400 gāthās); take one thematic run of 30.

### C. **Sāsanavaṃsa ch. 3 *Suvaṇṇabhūmi*** (Paññāsāmi, Burma 1861)
- Pāli: VRI CST `cscd/e0702n.nrf3.xml`; PD edition Bode 1897 (PTS; Bode †1922). English B. C. Law 1952 is **not** PD → English would be a Sarasvatī draft. Heaviest review load; listed for completeness.

## Public-domain evidence
Gray 1886 is pre-1929 (US) and Gray died well before 1955 (life+70) → PD worldwide. VRI text is the 1956 Sixth Council recension; VRI publishes CST for free redistribution — we cite and re-key by verse, as with the SuttaCentral samples. Sanskrit parallels (Cāṇakya / Hitopadeśa) are noted by Gray and are themselves PD.
