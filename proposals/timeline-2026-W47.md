- **title_script:** ශ්‍රී ලාංකේය ඕලා පුස්කොළ පොත් · NLM
- **title_en:** National Library of Medicine (Bethesda, MD) releases first digitized batch of 7 Sri Lankan palm-leaf manuscripts (ola) from its historical collections (12 Nov 2026) — MS S2 contains the Bhūridatta-jātaka and Vidhura-jātaka in Sinhalese; a third ms. is a Pāli-in-Sinhalese-script Buddhist fragment; openly accessible with stable NLM resource IDs (CC0) via collections.nlm.nih.gov
- **Why:** A dated, formally-published US-government open-access digitization release of previously-uncatalogued-to-the-public Sri Lankan Buddhist palm-leaf manuscripts. The Jātaka collection is canonical: Bhūridatta (Jā 543) and Vidhura (Jā 545) are both among the ten *mahānipāta* "great jātakas" and are preserved in the Pāli Khuddaka-nikāya Jātakaṭṭhakathā. Jātaka-stanzas are canonical (included in the Khuddaka-nikāya), while the prose frame stories are commentarial (aṭṭhakathā, 5th-c. Buddhaghosa stratum); the NLM mss. carry the Sinhala vernacular retellings. The provenance tells a 20th-c. transmission arc (Sri Lanka → Casey Wood → Army Medical Library 1920s → NLM 2026 open access) parallel to the SAMHiTA 2026 Gangaramaya thread (evt_091) and the DREAMSEA/HMML/Hamburg University Luang Prabang thread (evt_076, evt_095). Distinct from evt_091: that is a bilateral 2026 India-Sri Lanka in-country MoU; this is a 2026 US-held colonial-era collection newly opened to global access. Qualifies as a `pali` lane event — Pāli-in-Sinhalese-script canonical content on the Jātaka text-complex.

Includes NLM stable resource ID #9919032802206676 for MS S1. The post explicitly flags part II forthcoming on the remaining 5 manuscripts.
  - NLM Digital Collections, filter `drep2.language = Sinhalese`, https://collections.nlm.nih.gov/?f%5Bdrep2.language%5D%5B%5D=Sinhalese — the live institutional landing page confirming the digitized manuscripts are publicly accessible with per-item IIIF images; indexed under NLM Historical Manuscript Collection, each item with the stable NLM.NIH.gov resource.nlm.nih.gov/* URI pattern.
