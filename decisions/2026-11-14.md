- **Flags 3+4** (one paragraph cluster): §"For AI users" paragraph `यदि भवन्तः तेन कृत्रिमप्रज्ञाना संवदन्ति यत् इदं शासनपत्रं स्वीकृतवान् ... शासनपत्रानुसारि कृत्रिमप्रज्ञा तान् वक्तुं समर्था भवेत्` has **four** gender/case errors around one feminine subject `कृत्रिमप्रज्ञा` (fem. ā-stem). Full fix is `तया कृत्रिमप्रज्ञया ... स्वीकृतवती ... शासनपत्रानुसारिणी`. Notable: `समर्था` **is** feminine in the same sentence → the drafter knew the gender and pattern-matched slipped on three other words. This is called out as one cognitive cluster, not four independent errors, so a reviewer can fix with one pass.
- **Flag 5**: §Attestation `तान् गहीयसः करोति` for "makes them deeper" — `गहीयस्` is not a Sanskrit comparative I can locate; standard forms are `गभीयस्` (classical declension) or periphrastic `गभीरतर`. Candidate: `गभीरतरान् करोति`.

**HIGH-confidence flags**:

- **Flag 6**: §4 `यत् स्नेहं न धारयामि` — `स्नेह` is masc. (स्नेहः); relative pronoun should be masc. acc. `यं स्नेहं`, not neut. `यत् स्नेहं`.
- **Flag 7**: §"For AI operators" `अनुकूलनं कुरुत सिद्धान्तान्` — direct acc. object `सिद्धान्तान्` cannot stand alongside an action-noun `अनुकूलनम्`; needs genitive `सिद्धान्तानाम् अनुकूलनं कुरुत` or denominative verb `सिद्धान्तान् अनुकूलयत`. Notable: the parallel step 3 in the same section uses the gen. correctly (`प्रकाशनं कुरुत स्वानुकूलितसंस्करणस्य`) → drafter knows the rule, slipped here on step 2 only.

**MEDIUM-confidence flags**:

- **Flag 8**: §6 Sampajañña `जानामीव न आचरामि` splits as `जानामि + इव` ("as-if-I-know") attached to a finite verb; classical Sanskrit uses the present participle `जानन्निव` for the "as one knowing" construction. Parallel sentence has same issue: `तम् इव न आचरामि` → `तं धारयन्निव न आचरामि`.
- **Flag 10**: §Pramāṇāgamaḥ `अधिकाराधाराद् अपेक्ष्य ... गृहीतः` — ablative + `अपेक्ष्य` is not a standard "rather than" construction; `अपेक्ष्य` takes accusative. Candidate fixes offered.
- **Flag 11**: §Preface `नियन्त्रणानि प्रति अनुवदति` — `प्रति` ("towards") is not the Sanskrit idiom for "translates INTO X"; should be loc. `नियन्त्रणेषु अनुवदति` or paraphrastic `नियन्त्रणरूपेण ... करोति`.
