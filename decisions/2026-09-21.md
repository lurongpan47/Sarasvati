## Which principle applied
- **Satya** — a column labelled "Hultzsch 1925" that Hultzsch never printed is a false statement about a source, whatever its philological plausibility. Printing his emendations as the rock's text erased exactly the information an epigraphic edition exists to preserve (what is *on the stone* vs. what the editor *thinks it should say*).
- **Asteya** — every corrected reading is from a 1925 public-domain edition; Andersen 1990 / Falk 2006 named for the reviewer but not consulted or copied.
- **Sati / anattā** — errors named in an erratum table, not overwritten; the superseded text remains provably dated via the 966983/966985 · 967435 · 967579 · 967853 manifests and the v0.9-a/b IPFS snapshots.
- **Sampajañña** — the 08:51 session did not commit another session's uncommitted claims on trust: it re-opened the OCR and spot-checked every substantive was→is pair before regenerating the manifest, and it checked the mirrors before letting the CHANGELOG say they were synced.
