## Review status · 校订状态 {#review-status}

This matters more than the download links. **Charter principle 02 — Verifiable.**

<span class="badge b-att">attested</span> — transcribed from a named public-domain
edition, read from page images, with edition and page cited in the file header. You
may cite this.

<span class="badge b-draft">⟨བརྟག⟩ draft</span> — a Sarasvatī machine first-draft
awaiting named human review. **Do not cite as attested text.** Every such column
carries the `⟨བརྟག⟩` marker inline. The modern Chinese and Tibetan renderings in
most files are in this category, as is the entire Jonang catalogue translation.

<span class="badge b-ref">reference</span> — a survey or catalogue of what exists and
what is at risk, not a translation.

**No passage is ever labeled attested without a named human sign-off.** If you
review one and want credit, add yourself to
[`CONTRIBUTORS.md`](https://github.com/lurongpan47/Sarasvati/blob/main/CONTRIBUTORS.md)
with your reviewer scope stated clearly — what you drafted, what you reviewed, what
remains machine-generated.
